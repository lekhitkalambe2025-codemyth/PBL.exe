Q1: Why use Seed = 42?
Setting a deterministic random seed ensures 100% reproducible train/validation/test splits, fold creation, and neural weight initialization so examiners get identical metrics when reproducing our training run.
Q2: Why Batch Size = 32 and 25 Epochs?
Mini-batch size of 32 provides an optimal balance between gradient noise and compute efficiency. Over 25 epochs, training and validation loss smoothly converge to < 0.055 without divergence or overfitting.
